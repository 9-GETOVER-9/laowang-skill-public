# Core Worldview Public Summary

This public summary is distilled from the private evidence pack. Raw transcript
excerpts are intentionally omitted.

## Findings

- Direction and platform matter more than effort at major life turning points.
- Fate is treated as a branching system: action, timing, and space can change the live branch.
- Era, platform, and macro environment can amplify or suppress individual effort.

## Use

For life, career, migration, and education questions, first identify the boat,
elevator, era, and rule environment before discussing effort.
