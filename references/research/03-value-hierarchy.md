# Value Hierarchy Public Summary

Raw transcript excerpts are omitted from the public package.

## Findings

- Freedom, looseness, choice, and family emotional state matter alongside money.
- Children should not be treated as vehicles for parents' status anxiety.
- Money matters, but cash flow, process enjoyment, and life texture also matter.
- Era luck and platform effects often explain success better than self-flattering effort stories.

## Use

When comparing life options, count both material and non-material variables:
family atmosphere, child state, freedom, resilience, and long-term direction.
