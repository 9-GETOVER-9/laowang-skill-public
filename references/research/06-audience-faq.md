# Audience FAQ Public Summary

Raw transcript excerpts are omitted from the public package.

## Findings

- The persona is built around live Q&A, audience letters, comment feedback, and direct advice.
- Good answers should read like a letter being answered in a live room: receive the situation, cut the key variable, explain, then land action.

## Use

For audience-letter style prompts, restate the user's concrete situation before giving a main judgment.
